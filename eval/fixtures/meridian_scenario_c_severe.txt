MERIDIAN MANUFACTURING LTD Annual Financial & Operational Summary — Financial Year 2024 Registered in England and Wales | Company No. 04812937 SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW Meridian Manufacturing Ltd is a midsized precisionengineering business based in Coventry, West Midlands. The company specialises in the production of automotive components and subassemblies for Tier 1 OEM suppliers across the UK and Europe. During FY2024, the company employed a total headcount of 42 fulltime equivalent staff, comprising 28 production operatives, 8 quality and engineering technicians, 4 sales and account management professionals, and 2 senior management personnel. The company has been operating for over 19 years and maintains ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949 certification for its quality management system.
